# Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

## What FirstSeat is

FirstSeat helps you catch hard-to-book restaurant reservations. For each restaurant, we know the rule by which it releases its tables — how many days ahead, and at what time — and we alert you a few minutes before that window opens, with a direct link to book, shown in your own timezone.

## FirstSeat does not book tables for you

FirstSeat is an alerting tool, not a booking service. We tell you the exact moment a restaurant's reservations open and give you the link — but **you make the booking yourself**, directly on the restaurant's platform (Resy, OpenTable, DoorDash, and so on). FirstSeat never books, holds, or manages a reservation on your behalf, and never automates access to any booking platform. Completing a booking is up to you, subject to that platform's own terms.

## Your responsibilities

- You are responsible for keeping your account secure — protecting your email and password protects your FirstSeat account.
- You are responsible for actually booking the table when we alert you. An alert is a heads-up, not a guarantee that a table will be available.
- You agree to use FirstSeat for personal, non-commercial purposes, and not to abuse, overload, or attempt to disrupt the service.

## The information we rely on

We do our best to keep each restaurant's release rule accurate, based on what the restaurant publishes. Rules can change without notice, and platforms can behave unexpectedly. FirstSeat is provided **as-is**, without a guarantee that every alert will be perfectly timed or that a table will be available when a window opens.
