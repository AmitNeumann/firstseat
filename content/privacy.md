# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

## What we collect

When you create an account, we store your **email address**, your **name** (if you provide one), and your **timezone** — the timezone is what lets us show you each reservation's drop time on your own clock, not just New York's.

When you create a watch, we store the details you enter: the **restaurant**, the **date** you want to eat, the **party size**, and the **meal**. We use these to compute exactly when that restaurant's booking window opens and when to alert you.

## Who can see your data

Your watches are yours. Access is scoped to your account in our server code, so one user cannot see another user's watches — a request for someone else's data comes back as if it never existed. Your account details are visible only to you.

## What we do with it

We use your information only to run FirstSeat: to compute drop times, to show them in your timezone, and to alert you before a booking window opens. That's it.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your data, to anyone, ever.
- We don't show ads or use your data for advertising.
- We don't share your data with third parties, other than the infrastructure providers that run the service.

## Third parties

FirstSeat runs on a few infrastructure services, each of which processes data only as needed to provide it:

- **Supabase** — database and authentication.
- **Vercel** — hosting.
- **Resend** — sending the alert emails (when the alerting feature is enabled).

## Secrets and security

Sensitive keys (such as our email-sending API key) are stored as server-side environment variables, never exposed to the browser or committed to our code repository.

## This is a student project

FirstSeat is a demonstration project built for a university course, not a commercial service. We keep the data footprint small on purpose, but please don't store anything sensitive in it.
